## Refund and cancellation policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026. Last updated: 12 November 2026.

This policy explains how cancelling and refunds work for paid madani bot plans. It is part of our [terms of use](terms.md). Nothing here limits rights you have under the consumer law that applies to you.

The app itself is free for personal use, with every feature, on your own computer or server. You can try everything before you pay. A paid plan can add a madani cloud machine, a work license, or both: Plus ($20 a month) adds a madani cloud machine with 2 vCPU, 4 GB RAM and a 20 GB disk; Pro ($50 a month) adds a bigger one (4 vCPU, 8 GB RAM, 60 GB disk) with priority wake and a work license for one person; and Team ($119 a month) adds a dedicated machine (8 vCPU, 16 GB RAM, 160 GB disk) and a Team license with 2 seats, at $20 a month for each extra team member. A work license on its own is $12 a month. Yearly billing is two months free: Plus is $200 a year, Pro $500 and Team $1,190.

## Who bills you

Dodo Payments is our merchant of record. It takes the payment, charges any tax and issues your invoices. Refunds are paid by Dodo Payments to the card or payment method you used. Once a refund is issued, it usually reaches you within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Your AI provider bills you separately for AI usage under your own plan with them. We cannot refund AI provider charges. Ask that provider about them.

## Cancelling a plan

You can cancel at any time from the billing page of your account at madani.bot/dashboard. You do not need to contact us.

- Cancelling stops future renewals. You are not charged again.
- Your plan keeps working until the end of the period you have already paid for. That includes your madani cloud desktop and any work license in the plan.
- When the plan ends, bots on your own computer or server carry on. A madani cloud desktop is kept for 30 days and then deleted. We email you 7 days before it is deleted, so you can move your bots or save what you need.
- Closing the app, disconnecting a workspace or stopping a bot does not cancel a plan.
- Cancelling a madani bot plan does not cancel your AI provider subscriptions.

If you start the plan again before the desktop is deleted, it is kept.

## Refunds

**Your first payment.** If you are not happy with a new plan, email hello@madani.bot from the address on your account within 14 days of your first payment for that plan and we will refund that payment in full. We then end the plan and its madani cloud desktop.

**Yearly renewals.** If a yearly plan renews and you did not mean to keep it, email us within 14 days of the renewal and we will refund the renewal payment in full and end the plan.

**Monthly renewals.** Monthly renewals are not refunded. Cancel before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. The plan keeps working until the end of the month you have paid for.

**Changing plans.** Moving to a higher plan charges the difference for the rest of the current period at once. Moving to a lower plan takes effect without a refund for the time already paid.

**Our mistakes.** We always refund in full:

- a duplicate charge or a second subscription opened by mistake (these are refunded automatically when our system detects them);
- a charge made after you cancelled;
- a charge for the wrong amount;
- a period in which we could not provide your madani cloud desktop for a substantial part of the time because of a fault on our side. We refund that period, or the affected part of it.

**Accounts ended for abuse.** If we end a plan because of a serious breach of our [terms of use](terms.md) or [acceptable use policy](acceptable-use.md), we do not refund the current period, unless the law requires it.

## How to ask for a refund

Email hello@madani.bot from the email address on your account. Include the plan, the date of the charge and, if you have it, the invoice number from the billing page. Do not send card details. We reply within 3 business days.

If you think a charge is wrong, please contact us before disputing it with your bank. We can usually fix it faster.

## Changes to prices

Current prices are on the pricing page at madani.bot/pricing. If we change the price of a plan you are on, we will email you at least 30 days before it applies. The new price takes effect from your next renewal after that notice. You can cancel before then.
