madani bot is free for personal use, with every feature, on your own computer. you need an account only to buy a plan.

## plans

| | free | plus | pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **price** | $0, forever | $20 a month | $50 a month |
| **yearly** | | $200 a year | $500 a year |
| **every feature of the app** | yes | yes | yes |
| **[madani cloud](/docs/madani-cloud) computer** | no | 2 vcpu, 4 gb ram, 20 gb disk | 4 vcpu, 8 gb ram, 60 gb disk, priority wake |
| **work license** | add one, $12 a month | add one, $12 a month | yes, for one person |

yearly is two months free. **team** is $119 a month or $1,190 a year: a team license with 2 seats ($30) and a dedicated madani cloud with 8 vcpu, 16 gb ram and a 160 gb disk ($89), with priority wake. each extra team member is $20 a month, up to 50. add the team license yourself on the billing page; write to hello@madani.bot to set up the dedicated machine. current prices are on [the pricing page](/pricing).

**ai usage is never on your madani bill.** your ai provider bills you on your plan with them.

using madani bot at or for a business needs a work license. pro includes one for one person, or you can add one on its own for $12 a month (on free or plus) from the billing page of your account. the [license page](/license) has the exact terms.

## your account

go to [madani.bot/dashboard]({{app}}) and enter your email. we send a code; there's no password. the account shows your plan, invoices, and each place your bots live, with their bots, usage and bot computers. it never sees your messages, prompts, files or keys.

payments, tax and invoices are handled by dodo payments, our merchant of record. we never see your card number.

## changing or cancelling

change plan, switch to yearly, or cancel any time from the billing page of your account.

- **closing the app doesn't cancel a plan.** cancel on the billing page.
- **cancelling madani doesn't cancel your ai.** claude, chatgpt and the rest are separate.
- **when a plan ends,** bots on your own computer carry on. a cloud computer is kept for 30 days, then deleted (we email you a week before).

questions about a charge? write to hello@madani.bot from the email on your account.

## your data and deleting the account

the account page has **Download your data** and **delete account**. deleting it doesn't touch anything on your own computer or server, and an active plan has to be cancelled first (the page offers to). a cloud computer on the account is deleted right away, so download anything you want to keep first.
