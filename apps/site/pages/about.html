about

madani bot is a desktop app where you run a team of ai bots and text them like teammates.

{{strip cliffs | circle blue, heart pink, hex orange}}

the idea

one chat box is the wrong shape for getting work done. a few teammates {{bot circle blue}}{{bot hex orange}}{{bot star green}} is the right one. so every bot here gets a name, a job, a memory and a computer of its own.

three things we will not budge on.

your stuff stays yours

bots, chats, files and memory live on your computer or your own server. they only live with us if you choose a cloud computer, and you can move them back.

bring the ai you like

madani bot has no model of its own to sell you. you sign in with the one you already use, give each bot a different one if you want, and swap whenever something better comes out.

you stay in charge

bots ask before the scary stuff: sending, buying, deleting. you can watch a bot work on its computer and take the mouse back at any moment.

who makes it.

madani bot is independent. it is not owned by, paid for by or affiliated with any of the ai companies whose models it runs on. it is made by a very small team, with a lot of help from its own bots.

the app is source-available and free for personal use. using it for work needs a license. the license page has the details.

madani bot is a fork of openmausbot, by milind soni and its contributors. that original code stays under the apache 2.0 license, and we are grateful for it.

the fastest way to reach us is hello@madani.bot. a person reads every message.
